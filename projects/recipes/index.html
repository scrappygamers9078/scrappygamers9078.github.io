My Favorite Family Recipe!

Brigadeiro - A Brazilian Sensation

The Story

My mom was born and raised in Brazil, and she came to the United States when she was just 23. And she in came here in the hopes of an American dream she'd always hoped for, although she had a tough time being in a whole different country, with a different language, different people, and what felt like a whole new world thousands of miles away fom home. And as she settled in the U.S. she started feeling homesick fast, missing her friends, family and most of all the delcious foods in Brazil. And making Brazilian traditional food really helped her feel as if she was right back at home. And years later meeting an American man from New York and eventually falling in love, marrying, and having me, she had wanted me to have a connection with Brazil, as she had had her entire life, so what better way than cooking the foods she once ate as a child in Brazil? For my 1st Birthday, she made Brigadeiro, for my 2nd Birthday she made Bridgadeiro, and so on, every year on my birthday she makes Brigadeiro, but just for my birthday. Every chance she gets she makes Brigadeiro in some way, like on her boss's birthday she made a Brigadeiro cake. And I asked her why, why do you always make Brigadeiro for occasions, and she said, "because that's what we do in Brazil."
