Beijinho

Instructions

Making Brigderio is actually quite easy and simple, take ALL your ingredents with the correct measurement and put it all in a pot over mediuim heat, mix until it reaches a thick consistencity, or put over mediuim heat for 10 minutes while stirring. When done, take a bowl of chocolate coconut flakes and take a ball of the Beijinho liquid we just made and roll it around in the coconut flakes, until it's covered all around with the coconut flakes.

Ingredient Measurements
Coconut Flakes 2 Spoons
Condensed Milk 1 Can
Butter 2 Spoons
