Brigadeiro

Ingredient Measurements
Cocoa Powder 2 Spoons
Condensed Milk 1 Can
Butter 2 Spoons

Making Brigderio is actually quite easy and simple, take ALL your ingredents with the correct measurement and put it all in a pot over mediuim heat, mix until it reaches a thick consistencity, or put over mediuim heat for 10 minutes while stirring. When done, take a bowl of chocolate sprinkles and take a ball of the Brigaderio chocolate we just made and roll it around in the sprinkles, until it's covered all around with the sprinkles.
